Add Spotify history

Back to the dashboard

1. Ask Spotify for your history

  1. On spotify.com, sign in and open your account's Privacy settings.
  2. Under Download your data, tick Extended streaming history and request it. It covers your whole account; Spotify says it can take up to 30 days.
  3. When Spotify emails you, download the zip file to this phone.

2. Choose the file here

Pick the zip file, or the JSON files inside it. Everything is read on this phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

3. Upload it to your repository

  1. Open your listening-dashboard repository on github.com and go into the data folder. (It appears after the first update has run.)
  2. Press Add file, then Upload files, and choose spotify-history.json from your downloads.
  3. Press Commit changes. An update starts by itself. When it shows a green check in the Actions tab, reload the dashboard.

To update it later, request a new download from Spotify, make the file again here and upload it the same way; it replaces the old one. Plays that Last.fm already has are left out automatically, so nothing counts twice.

Like your Last.fm history, this file is readable by anyone who finds your public repository. It holds only times, seconds played, artists, songs and albums.
